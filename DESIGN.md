## Overview

Context: **fabre-DesignSystem**, **light** theme, **large web** (`web/lg`).

The YAML and the marked quick-reference tables are generated snapshots of `packages/tokens/dtcg/`, not a separate token source. Colour keys retain palette and semantic role paths; typography keys identify complete text-style composites. Use the named tokens in implementation, not values to hardcode. Computed foregrounds and elevation retain their canonical token definitions instead of reimplementing their colour or shadow policies here; the documentation view resolves them from project CSS. Keep generated regions intact when editing design intent, and regenerate them after a reviewed token change. Other contexts must not reuse these values blindly. Validate all document contexts with `bun run design:lint`.

Other adopted brand and theme contexts:

- [fabre-DesignSystem — dark, large web](design/fabre-designsystem/dark.md)

Make the product recognisable and useful; keep brand expression in service of comprehension.
