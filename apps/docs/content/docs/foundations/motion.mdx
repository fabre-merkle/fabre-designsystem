## Our motion

The way an interface responds, reveals information and connects moments contributes to the
brand. Motion should support the task and make change easier to understand, not compete for
attention.

- **Intentional:** Give movement a reason, such as explaining change or confirming an action.
- **Coherent:** Let movement feel related across the experience and its visual language.
- **Considerate:** Keep motion comfortable and predictable; the experience must remain
  understandable without it.
{/* topic:end motion-principles */}

## When to animate

Prefer brief, unobtrusive movement for routine feedback. Give larger changes enough time to
be understood, and avoid movement that delays a task or repeatedly draws attention.
{/* topic:end motion-usage */}

{/* topic:start motion-accessibility-link */}
## Respect motion preferences

Keep information and feedback available without non-essential movement. Respect a person's
reduced-motion preference, and avoid constant or unexpected animation.
{/* topic:end motion-accessibility-link */}
