## Our brand

The design system should make the product recognisable and useful across touchpoints. Begin
with the problem the product solves and the benefit it promises, then use consistent design
decisions to support that purpose. Keep expression in service of comprehension: a distinctive
interface should still make the next action clear.
{/* topic:end brand-essence */}

{/* topic:start brand-personality */}
## Brand attributes

Attributes turn brand character into choices people can recognise. Clear, considerate,
dependable and empowering are brand-neutral starting points; refine their expression when
project guidance is available. Each describes tone and voice, the interface across senses and
agent-mediated interactions, and the experience people should have.
{/* topic:end brand-personality */}

{/* topic:start brand-attribute-clear */}
## Clear

Make meaning and intent easy to understand.

- **Tone and voice:** Name the action and its outcome. “Save changes” is clearer than “Go”.
- **Interface:** Make messages and actions recognisable whether someone sees, hears or feels
  the interface, or an agent interprets it on their behalf.
- **Experience:** Understanding comes before action. People should feel oriented, not left
  to guess what is being asked.
{/* topic:end brand-attribute-clear */}

{/* topic:start brand-attribute-considerate */}
## Considerate

Respect people's circumstances, attention and ways of interacting.

- **Tone and voice:** Help without blame. “Enter a delivery address” explains what is needed
  without saying “You forgot your address”.
- **Interface:** Keep the brand recognisable without relying on one sense or input method.
  Use sound, touch and movement with care.
- **Experience:** People should feel included and respected, not tested or pressured.
{/* topic:end brand-attribute-considerate */}

{/* topic:start brand-attribute-dependable */}
## Dependable

Make the brand's expression match what the product can actually do.

- **Tone and voice:** State outcomes accurately; distinguish “Saving changes” from
  “Changes saved”.
- **Interface:** Give progress, completion and failure consistent meanings across direct and
  agent-mediated interactions. Never imply success while the outcome is uncertain.
- **Experience:** Trust comes from promises and outcomes agreeing, including when a task
  cannot be completed.
{/* topic:end brand-attribute-dependable */}

{/* topic:start brand-attribute-empowering */}
## Empowering

Help people make informed choices and remain in control.

- **Tone and voice:** Explain consequences without pressure. Say “You can change this later”
  only when that is genuinely possible.
- **Interface:** Make choices, consequences and recovery understandable; assistance must not
  conceal alternatives or imply consent.
- **Experience:** People should feel capable and in charge. Assistance supports their intent
  rather than deciding it for them.
{/* topic:end brand-attribute-empowering */}

{/* topic:start brand-visual-style */}
## Expression across touchpoints

Carry the attributes through language, visual style, sound, touch and agent-mediated
interactions. Adapt the expression to its context without changing what the attribute means.
The attributes establish intended character; writing conventions and product decisions have
their own guidance rather than a second set of rules here.
{/* topic:end brand-visual-style */}
