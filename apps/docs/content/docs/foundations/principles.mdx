## Principles

These principles describe how the design system itself is made.

- **Composable.** Small parts combine into larger ones; try composition before adding a new
  component.
- **Accessible by default.** Keyboard, focus and assistive-technology support are built in.
- **Themeable.** Shared design decisions let a brand or mode change reach the whole system.
- **Lightweight.** Each part does one job and carries only what that job needs.
- **Owned.** The source lives in this project, where the team can understand and change it.
- **Well documented.** Each decision has one owning page for people and agents.

## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Primitive | An unstyled building block that supplies behaviour and accessibility. |
| Component | A styled, ready-to-use element that applies the system's decisions. |
| Block | A larger composition of components that solves a common product pattern. |
| Variant | A named, supported alternative of a component, such as its size or emphasis. |
