Surface levels describe fill and edge relative to the surrounding surface. Shadow levels
describe floating weight independently; nesting does not automatically increase shadow.
Recessed surfaces are dips, not negative elevation.

<TokenReference category="elevation" />

## Applying depth

Choose a surface level for the relationship it needs to express, rather than a neutral ramp
step. Use the matching edge on a surface and a recessed role for an inset region. Give
floating surfaces a deliberate shadow without making every nested element heavier.

Stacking-order and shadow-headroom values have no standalone visual preview. They are
shown as data rather than as an invented swatch.
