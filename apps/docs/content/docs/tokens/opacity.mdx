Opacity controls how strongly a layer or element is visible. Choose a semantic role for its
purpose and verify the resulting combination against the surface beneath it.

<TokenReference category="opacity" />

## Applying opacity

Use transparency deliberately for de-emphasis or overlays, not as the only indication of
state. Text and controls still need sufficient contrast after compositing.
