Use the spacing scale for relationships between elements and the control roles for a
control's internal gaps, padding, height and icon size. Brand breakpoints and grid dimensions
are separate decisions, not inferred from the spacing scale.

<TokenReference category="spacing-sizing" />

## Applying the scale

Use smaller gaps to connect related items and larger gaps to distinguish groups and
sections. Keep a control's internal spacing proportionate to its size. Interactive targets
need at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels or sufficient spacing around them, as required by WCAG
2.2 criterion 2.5.8.
