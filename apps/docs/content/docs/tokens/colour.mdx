Choose a semantic role for its intended use rather than a convenient ramp step. Primary,
secondary and tertiary identify brand families, not control priorities. Pair each fill with
its intended foreground, and do not rely on colour alone to communicate status.

The values and previews below follow the selected brand and show light and dark modes
side by side.

## Pairing and accessibility

Use the matching on-brand role for text or icons on a brand fill, and the matching
on-surface role on a quiet branded surface. Apply the same principle to intent colours and
their foregrounds. Check each pairing in every supported mode: a named pair is not, by
itself, a guarantee of adequate contrast. Keep control boundaries and focus indicators
perceivable.
