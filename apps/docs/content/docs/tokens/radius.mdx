title: Radius
description: Corner radius and stroke-width values.
---

Choose a radius for the element's purpose and size. Use the small-elements role for compact
shapes, the large-elements role for larger surfaces, and the full role for circular or pill
shapes. Stroke widths describe boundaries and focus, independently of corner shape.

<TokenReference category="radius" />

## Applying shape

Keep nested corners visually coherent and respect the project's inset minimum. Radius alone
does not communicate state; use a clear label, colour, icon or other cue as appropriate.
