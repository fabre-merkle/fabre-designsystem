{/* topic:start accessibility-target */}
## Conformance target

**WCAG 2.2 Level AA is the baseline accessibility target.** Confirm any additional legal,
organisational or product requirements for the people and markets the product serves.
Accessibility is part of the definition of done, not a final visual check.

For text legibility, also target **APCA Silver** as a design-quality measure. It complements
WCAG AA; it is not a replacement standard, a legal compliance claim or a guarantee of
accessibility.
{/* topic:end accessibility-target */}

{/* topic:start accessibility-contrast */}
## Contrast and legibility

Follow the APCA Readability Criterion's Silver guidance, including font qualification,
size-and-weight lookup, applicable minimum sizes and additional support requirements. Assess
the actual font, polarity and reading context: Silver is not a single contrast-score threshold.

The token system starts from absolute lightness-contrast values (Lc) of at least 75 for body
and control text, and at least 60 for large text. These are starting points, not proof of
Silver conformance. Where the actual size and weight need stronger contrast, follow the
applicable lookup guidance.

Check WCAG AA independently: normal text needs at least 4.5:1 and large text at least 3:1.
Meaningful non-text indicators and component boundaries need at least 3:1 where WCAG's
non-text contrast criterion applies. Check rendered foreground and background pairs in every
supported mode and state, along with line length, spacing, zoom and text reflow. Do not rely
on colour alone to communicate status or an action.
{/* topic:end accessibility-contrast */}

{/* topic:start accessibility-keyboard-focus */}
## Keyboard and focus

Make every interactive element reachable and operable with a keyboard. Preserve a visible
focus indicator, use a logical focus order and return focus appropriately after temporary
surfaces close. Avoid keyboard traps.
{/* topic:end accessibility-keyboard-focus */}

{/* topic:start accessibility-assistive-technology */}
## Assistive technology

Give controls meaningful accessible names, use appropriate native semantics and ensure
status changes are announced when needed. Informative images need an equivalent description;
decorative images should not add noise.
{/* topic:end accessibility-assistive-technology */}

{/* topic:start accessibility-reduced-motion */}
## Reduced motion

Respect the user's reduced-motion preference. Remove or replace non-essential movement while
preserving the information and feedback the interaction provides.
{/* topic:end accessibility-reduced-motion */}

{/* topic:start accessibility-testing */}
## Testing

Combine automated checks with keyboard, zoom and screen-reader testing. Automation can find
common failures but cannot establish whether the experience is understandable. Record WCAG
checks and APCA text-legibility checks separately, including the rendered mode, size and
weight tested.
{/* topic:end accessibility-testing */}

## International standards and regional requirements

WCAG is a shared technical foundation; countries and regions have their own laws, policies
and standards. This orientation list is not exhaustive or a legal compliance matrix. Confirm
the applicable version, sector, scope and obligations with the relevant specialists.

| Region | Standards and policies to investigate |
| --- | --- |
| International | [W3C WCAG](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) |
| United States | [Section 508](https://www.section508.gov/) |
| European Union | EN 301 549 and the [European Accessibility Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj) |
| Canada | [Accessible Canada Act](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/accessible-canada.html) and AODA in Ontario |
| United Kingdom | Equality Act 2010 and [public sector accessibility regulations](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/accessibility-requirements-for-public-sector-websites-and-apps) |
| Australia | [Disability Discrimination Act](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A04426/latest/text) |
| Japan | JIS X 8341-3 |
| India | [Guidelines for Indian Government Websites](https://guidelines.india.gov.in/) |
| China | [GB/T 37668](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/gb/newGbInfo?hcno=35ECC696805C1A67C93B74FB6D0D8EFB) |

Do not infer that every listed law mandates the same WCAG version or that the baseline
satisfies every regional obligation.

## Prior art

- [Salesforce Lightning Design System global accessibility standards](https://www.lightningdesignsystem.com/2e1ef8501/p/23a1dd-global-accessibility-standards)
  informed the regional orientation list; current primary sources take precedence.
- [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) is the AA conformance baseline.
- [APCA](https://git.apcacontrast.com/) and the draft
  [APCA Readability Criterion](https://readtech.org/ARC/tests/visual-readability-contrast/?tn=criterion)
  inform the additional Silver legibility target, independently of WCAG conformance.
